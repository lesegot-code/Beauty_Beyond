A Beauty Beyond Compare

A birthday wish, inspired by William Shakespeare

✿

Your eyes need not outshine the morning sun, Nor must your smile make roses fade away; For beauty needs no contest to be won, Nor borrowed stars to brighten up the day.

✿

I've yet to solve the mystery in your gaze, That quiet look that leaves me wondering still; Some charms are best admired without a phrase, And some leave warmth no words can quite fulfil.

✿

Your gentle voice turns simple words to gold, "Ke siame, o jang?" — I love that sound; For sweetest things need neither fame nor gold, But leave a little joy wherever they're found.

✿

And though I know you not in every way, There's something lovely in your quiet grace; A charm that needs no grand words of display, A light that shines without seeking its place.

✿

So may this day bring laughter, joy, and light, And fill your heart with memories to treasure; May dreams that once seemed distant come in sight, And may your days be rich with simple pleasure.

✿

May fortune smile upon the paths you choose, May hope remain when skies are painted grey; May life bring more than you could dare to lose, And give you brighter reasons every day.

✿

I need no stars to tell me you are rare, Nor roses dressed in words of grand acclaim; For there's a quiet beauty in your air, That makes you lovely without praise or fame.

✿

So here's to you, and all that lies ahead, To joys unseen and dreams yet to pursue; May kindness crown each path on which you tread, And may the coming year be good to you.

Happy birthday.
