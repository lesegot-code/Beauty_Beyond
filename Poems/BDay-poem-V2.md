# A Beauty Beyond Compare

<small>Inspired by Shakespeare's Sonnet 130, written for someone worth knowing.</small>

---
Your eyes need not outshine the morning sun,

Nor must your smile make roses fade away;

For beauty needs no contest to be won,

Nor borrowed stars to brighten up the day.

---
I've yet to solve the mystery in your gaze,

That quiet look which leaves me searching still;

And though I've known you only for a while,

You stir a warmth no words could quite fulfil.

---
Your gentle voice turns simple words to gold,

*“Ke siame, o jang?”* — I love that sound;

And when you roll your eyes at jokes I've told,

I find there's charm in mischief to be found.

---
So on this day, no grander words I'll use;

No goddess, rose, or star could capture you.

---
For what makes you so lovely isn't borrowed —

It's simply, wonderfully, being you.
