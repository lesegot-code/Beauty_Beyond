A little Shakespeare, a little you ❤️
Your eyes need not outshine the morning sun,
Nor must your smile outshine the flowers in spring;
For beauty isn't measured, second to none,
Nor does it need a thousand songs to sing.

I cannot claim to know your every part,
For time has only just begun our tale;
Yet something kind has stirred within my heart,
A little warmth no passing days can pale.

So on this day, I wish you joy and light,
May laughter find you more than once today;
May all your dreams seem closer in your sight,
And may good things accompany your way.

I need no stars to tell me you are rare,
For there's a charm in simply being you;
And though I know you not beyond compare,
I'm glad our paths have crossed, and hope yours bloom anew.
